The Napkin

Version 1

Welcome to The Napkin, a Classical Blog for those who miss the rough and ineffable writing that comes from humans alone. Born out of the vertigo, TV-static, heart-drop of realizing you don’t know how much of what you’re reading is an LLM-agent—if there’s any content left with only another person behind it.

The Napkin guarantees authentic, messy, decidedly human writing. Anyone writing for The Napkin must first read this aloud

I hereby pledge that I will write from my heart, ideas that are mine, that inspire me, and bring something new into the world. I will not use AI as I write—not to find ideas, not to generate text, not even to format or smooth out my writing. I will be direct, I will not over-polish, and I will write to the people.

To be clear, The Napkin does not enforce or endorse any specific views on science and AI. We are not against the multitude of interesting ways people have discovered to use LLMs, including in writing. Rather, we want one space that is free from vibe-writing and the style that goes along with that, where everything you read promises time, thought, and personality.

--From those who miss content to those who do as well.
