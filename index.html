The Napkin

Welcome to *The Napkin,* a “publication” for humans. Sketching, ideating, casting spells. It was born out of a nostalgia for reading genuine, good faith content. Think (parts of) the early internet, or letters between far-flung collaborators; a conversation with a friend. I miss reading something I know someone spent a lot of time thinking about, and didn’t agonize about polishing. Voilà: *The Napkin.*

How do you make something that captures this? Simple. Anyone writing on *The Napkin* is forced to read the following aloud:

*I hereby pledge that I will not use AI as I write—not to find my ideas, not to generate text, not even to make my formatting beautiful. I will write from my own heart and I will write to the people.*

To be clear, *The Napkin* does not enforce or endorse any specific views on Science and AI. Simply, it is one space we promise is free from vibe-written and the style that goes along with that, where everything takes time and thought.

From those who miss this to those who do as well.
