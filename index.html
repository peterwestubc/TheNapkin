The Napkin

Welcome to The Napkin, a “publication” for humans. Sketching, ideating, casting spells. It was born out of a nostalgia for reading genuine, good faith content. Think (parts of) the early internet, or letters between far-flung collaborators; a conversation with a friend. I miss reading something I know someone spent a lot of time thinking about, free from the shiny formatting of modern blogs and delegation to LLM agents. Voilà: The Napkin.

How do you make something that captures this? Simple. Anyone on The Napkin is forced to read the following aloud before writing:

I hereby pledge that I will not use AI as I write—not to find my ideas, not to generate text, not even to make my formatting beautiful. I will write from my own heart and I will write to the people.

To be clear, The Napkin does not enforce or endorse any specific views on science and AI. We are not against the multitude of interesting ways people have discovered to use LLMs, including in writing. Rather, this provides one space free from vibe-writing and the style that goes along with that, where everything you read promises time, thought, and personality.

--From those who miss this to those who do as well.
