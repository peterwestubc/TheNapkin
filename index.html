The Napkin

Welcome to The Napkin, a “publication” for humans, by humans; for sketching, ideating, and casting spells. The Napkin was born out of burnout from the arms race of academic and intellectual writing: more complex terms, better formatting and figures, finding increasingly extravagant and subtle ways to make the most of your LLM agents, and always this feeling that a corporate PR team had the final say. The dream of The Napkin is one space for genuine, messy, decidedly human content, guaranteed by the authors and the creators.

To ensure this spirit, anyone writing for The Napkin must first read this aloud:

I hereby pledge that I will write from my heart, ideas that are mine, that inspire me, and bring something new into the world. I will not use AI as I write—not to find ideas, generate text, or event format my piece. I will be direct, I will not over-polish, and I will write to the people.

To be clear, The Napkin does not enforce or endorse any specific views on science and AI. We are not against the multitude of interesting ways people have discovered to use LLMs, including in writing. Rather, we want to summon content that is free from vibe-writing and the style that goes along with that, where everything you read promises time, thought, and personality.

--From those who miss content to those who do as well.
